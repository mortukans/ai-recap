# v1 — scroll-driven hero with layered parallax

- Concept: a tall pinned (sticky) hero whose timeline is scrubbed by scroll, not time. Five depth layers (amber glow + sound rings + giant waveform, giant italic Newsreader ghost word, a 3D-tilted HTML/CSS iPhone, floating UI cards/chips/Apple Watch, the red record button) move, scale and fade at different rates through 4 scenes: Record -> Transcript -> Recap -> Calendar. The bottom dock is the app's pill tab bar + red record button; it shows the scene and jumps to it. Then: scroll-linked marquee, features, privacy, plans, App Store CTA.
- Tech: one static index.html, vanilla JS + CSS, no libraries, no images (everything is HTML/CSS/inline SVG). rAF loop eases a progress value and writes CSS custom properties; CSS only consumes them in transform/opacity.
- Real: all product facts, prices, links, brand tokens and fonts come from BRIEF.md / the app's lv.ts / the store listing. EN + LV (toggle, navigator.language, localStorage, ?lang=), light + dark (?theme=).
- Placeholder / illustrative: the phone and watch screens are hand-built mocks with the generic fictional "Q4 roadmap review" content (no designer sample names); the "Download on the App Store" badge is a plain text badge, not Apple's artwork.
- Reduced motion (or ?motion=off): the hero stays pinned but scenes swap by a short cross-fade only - no parallax, tilt, marquee or ambient animation; hero is shorter.
- Debug hooks: ?s=0..3 (scene) or ?p=0..1 (hero progress) scroll to that state; add &still to render it without scrolling (needed for headless Chrome, which paints blank after a programmatic scroll). ?at=<section id> pulls a section to the top for capture.
- Mobile (<900px): copy on top, phone bleeds off the bottom, one floating card per scene, fewer background layers.
- Polish pass 2026-11-12: favicon (svg + 32px png + apple-touch-icon from the app icon), Open Graph/Twitter tags + `og.png` (og:image assumes the page is served from https://mortukans.github.io/ai-recap/), App Store smart-banner meta, Support mailto in the footer. Languages: positioned as "built for Latvian + English, even mixed — Russian and other languages work too; the app UI is LV + EN" (hero, meta description, feature card, marquee). Sample meeting made consistent with the November 2026 calendar (ship by end of November, analytics to December, sync "Tuesday at ten", 90-min recording limit shown). Privacy block now says audio is sent to the AI provider for processing only. Floating cards moved off the phone's text. Preview: `ai-recap-landing` in `.claude/launch.json` serves `landing/` on :4173.
- Known gaps: phone-width screenshots were taken through _shots/frame.html (an iframe) because headless Chrome cannot lay out narrower than ~500px; not tested on a physical iPhone/Safari. _shots/ and frame.html are verification artefacts, not part of the page.
