Features

Everything around the recording.

Records with the screen locked

The timer lives in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, with pause and finish buttons.

Contexts that speak your vocabulary

Work meeting, sales call, interview, lecture, personal note — or your own, with vocabulary and instructions.

Start from your wrist

Start a recording on Apple Watch. The watch can record on its own and hand the audio to the phone.

Ask the recording

“What did we decide on pricing?” The answer cites the moment in the transcript.

Latvian, English — and more

Built for Latvian and English — even when both land in one sentence. Conversations in Russian and other languages are transcribed too. The app itself is in Latvian and English.

Share it anywhere

Apple Notes, Mail or Notion with formatting intact. Export Markdown or txt.

Privacy

Private by default.

Your conversations are yours. AI Recap is built so they stay with you.

Stored on your device

Audio, transcripts and recaps are stored only on your iPhone, in an encrypted database.

Processed, not kept

For transcription and the recap, audio is sent to the AI provider for processing only. We don't store it.

No ads, no tracking

The app has no ads and no tracking.
